        # Projects: 25 years of research, from theory to the patient

        These projects trace my research path: my PhD at Inria (2001-2005), my postdoc in Uppsala (2005-2006), then twenty years as an associate professor at [Université Grenoble Alpes](https://www.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/) ([TIMC](https://www.timc.fr/) laboratory, [GMCAO](https://www.timc.fr/GMCAO) team). This path went from theory to application. The early work, in discrete geometry and image analysis, laid mathematical and algorithmic foundations. The later projects started from problems brought by clinicians, with one goal: getting back to the patient's bedside.

        To reach that goal, I worked with clinicians, MedTech companies and funders (ANR, Carnot, SATT Linksium). The diagram places each project on the technology readiness scale (TRL): each column's height shows how far it travelled toward the patient, up to trials on healthy volunteers for the LPR robot.

        This path toward the patient is what I now want to take further, alongside companies. What I take from it, and bring to my clients: decide on evidence, prototype just what is needed, document for what comes next.
