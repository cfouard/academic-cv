Medical software rarely fails for technical reasons alone. It fails because the clinical need was poorly framed, a technical barrier was found too late, or the regulatory cost was not anticipated. My job: give you **the evidence to decide before committing to development**, then help you carry it out.

## Who is it for?

- **Medical device SMEs** whose clinicians ask for a software feature the team cannot assess.
- **Medical software SMEs** launching a new product without the right project lead yet.
- **MedTech start-ups** that must keep their roadmap without an experienced CTO.
- **Technology transfer offices and incubators** deciding whether to invest in a research-born medical software project.

## Project assessment

An independent, reasoned assessment on two axes: **clinical need** and **technical feasibility**.

**What you get:**

- a clinical need checked with several clinicians and written up as functional specifications (validated with you mid-way);
- a state of the art and an analysis of technical barriers, with several possible solutions for each;
- an analysis of data and integration (imaging, hospital systems, existing code);
- an indicative estimate of the software class (IEC 62304, MDR), which drives the cost of CE marking;
- a prototype estimate in person-days and profiles;
- a written report and an oral debrief.

**Duration:** from a few weeks to about three months, depending on the project's maturity. A half-day **pre-assessment** sizes the assessment precisely; it is deducted if the assessment is ordered.

The decision is yours: I give you a precise evaluation, not a convenient go/no-go. And the next step is never automatic.

## Part-time R&D leadership

About **one day a week** to frame and steer the development of a new medical software product with your team: technical choices, link with clinicians, product priorities, and a working method handed over to your team.

## Next steps, if the project goes on

- **De-risking prototype**, built with your team on the open-source [CamiTK](../projects/camitk/) platform or your own framework, including team training.
- **Transfer package for product development**: requirements, architecture, software risk analysis, traceability; documentation structured according to IEC 62304, ISO 14971 and IEC 62366-1, reusable in your technical file.

## Out of scope

CE marking, ISO 13485 quality system, full MDR technical file, notified body relations and hardware. I work upstream of these steps and can point you to partners.
